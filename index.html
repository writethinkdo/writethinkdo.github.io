From Thought to Practice: The WTD Framework

We reject pre-packaged consulting playbooks. Every mandate is engineered through our disciplined three-phase doctrine designed to turn complex strategic intent into durable operational realities.

Phase 01 • Clarify

Write: Precision Framing

Finding the exact words that define objectives, constraints, and commercial value. We cut through organizational fog to articulate underlying interests, test core assumptions, and establish indisputable problem boundaries.

Phase 02 • Analyze

Think: Applied Game Theory

Dogged quantitative and qualitative inquiry. Applying game theory and strategic scenario modeling, we evaluate counterparty payoffs, model vendor cost structures, and stress-test strategic options before table discussions commence.

Phase 03 • Execute

Do: Disciplined Intervention

Hands-on execution where rubber meets the road. Whether leading multi-party executive alignment sessions, sitting at the negotiation table, or implementing procurement controls, we ensure right things get done right.

Advisory Governance

Frequently Addressed Inquiries

Direct answers on our engagement structure, confidentiality protocols, and rapid deployment readiness.

How does WTD work alongside our internal legal and procurement teams?

We serve as an agile strategic force multiplier, not a replacement. In negotiations and procurement, we focus on commercial strategy, applied game theory, economic leverage modeling, and operational term structure. General Counsel and internal legal teams retain full authority over drafting and legal compliance, while Chief Procurement Officers leverage our market intelligence and objective third-party authority to strengthen their bargaining position.

What is your engagement structure for urgent or time-sensitive mandates?

When an imminent contract renewal deadline looms or an emergency board deadlock threatens strategic momentum, we can mobilize within 24 to 48 hours. Our senior partners immediately initiate game-theoretic scenario modeling, term audits, or stakeholder discovery to establish rapid tactical footing.

Are all discussions and preliminary consultations covered under confidentiality?

Unconditionally. High-stakes negotiations, procurement overhauls, and board mediations inherently deal with sensitive, market-moving, or politically delicate information. We routinely execute mutual non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) prior to exploratory briefings, and all client communications are handled with strict executive discretion.

Do you have track record advising public sector entities on statutory procurements?

Yes. Our Canadian clients include federal ministries, provincial agencies, and municipalities. We specialize in designing transparent, defensible RFx processes compliant with public sector procurement regulations and statutory transparency requirements, ensuring complete audit defensibility and mitigating the risk of vendor bid protests.

Can WTD provide behind-the-scenes negotiation coaching without sitting at the table?

Frequently. Many executive clients prefer that counterparties remain unaware of outside advisory support. In this game-theoretic advisory model, our advisors apply the Harvard Program on Negotiation (PON) mutual gains framework to prep your internal negotiating team between sessions, analyze counterparty offers, craft tactical response language, and map concession trades while your executives lead the public discussion.
