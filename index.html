How We Work

From Thought to Practice: The WTD Framework

We avoid off-the-shelf consulting playbooks. Every engagement follows a disciplined three-phase approach designed to turn strategic intent into lasting, real-world results.

Phase 01 • Clarify

Write: Clear Definition & Objectives

Finding the exact words that define goals, constraints, and commercial value. We cut through organizational clutter to uncover real priorities, test key assumptions, and clearly define what success looks like.

Phase 02 • Analyze

Think: Analysis & Scenario Modeling

Thorough analytical rigour. Applying practical game theory and scenario modeling, we assess counterparty positions, analyze cost structures, and pressure-test options before discussions or negotiations begin.

Phase 03 • Execute

Do: Practical Execution

Hands-on execution where it matters most. Whether facilitating executive alignment sessions, advising at the negotiation table, or putting procurement controls in place, we make sure the right things get done right.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About How We Work

Straightforward answers about our engagement model, confidentiality standards, and how we get started.

How does WTD work alongside our internal legal and procurement teams?

We work as an experienced strategic partner alongside your internal teams, not as a replacement. In negotiations and procurement, we focus on commercial strategy, applied game theory, cost modeling, and business terms. Your General Counsel and legal team maintain full authority over legal drafting, while procurement leaders use our external market insights and objective perspective to strengthen their negotiating position.

What is your approach for urgent or time-sensitive situations?

When facing a tight contract deadline or an urgent board deadlock, we can mobilize within 24 to 48 hours. Our senior partners immediately review key terms, model scenarios, and conduct rapid stakeholder briefings to build an effective action plan.

Are all discussions and preliminary consultations covered under confidentiality?

Yes, completely. Commercial negotiations, procurement projects, and executive mediations involve sensitive, often confidential information. We routinely sign mutual non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) before introductory discussions, and all client matters are handled with strict discretion.

Do you have experience advising public sector entities on regulated procurements?

Yes. Our Canadian clients include federal departments, provincial agencies, and municipal governments. We specialize in designing clear, competitive procurement processes that meet all public sector rules and transparency standards, ensuring complete audit readiness and preventing bid protests.

Can WTD provide behind-the-scenes negotiation advice without sitting at the table?

Frequently. Many clients prefer to keep outside advisory support behind the scenes. In this advisory model, we use the Harvard Program on Negotiation (PON) framework to prepare your internal team between sessions, evaluate counterparty proposals, draft counter-positions, and structure trade-offs while your executives lead the direct discussions.
